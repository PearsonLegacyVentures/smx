Private massage planning in Maho and Cupecoy

Maho and Cupecoy are Dutch-side resort and residential areas close to the airport corridor. A large beachfront resort, private suite and gated condo each have their own guest access rules.

Accommodation access

Airport-area traffic, resort security and condominium check-in procedures can affect visiting professionals. Supply the full resort or building name and whether there is a private treatment area away from public beach crowds.

Treatments for your visit

Individual post-travel relaxation massage may be requested; couples sessions need enough space and confirmed practitioner availability. Resort spa restrictions or condo security may prevent an external provider from entering.

Travel & local rules

Give the actual Maho or Cupecoy address, especially when arriving by air or staying at a gated resort. Do not assume the same travel window as a property in Philipsburg or Marigot.
