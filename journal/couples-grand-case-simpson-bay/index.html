Couples massage in Grand Case or Simpson Bay: the details

Simultaneous private treatments require two qualified practitioners and safe space.

A simultaneous couples session typically needs two available professionals, two tables and a room large enough for both guests. Staffing cannot be assumed.

Start with your exact accommodation

A property manager may need to approve a visiting professional. Share a specific approved room, the entrance and the island side.

Make a realistic request

Provide the guest count, service, preferred date at least six hours ahead and any access requirements. The Tranquilitas concierge checks qualifications and staffing.

Confirm pricing before payment

The complete service price, travel and property conditions must be agreed before an appointment is offered.
