FAQ

Questions before booking?

Our requests are transparent about what can be confirmed and what still needs checking.

Is this a Tranquilitas walk-in spa?

No. Tranquilitas was founded in Nassau, The Bahamas. This is an online mobile massage booking site, not a local walk-in spa.

Do you serve both the French and Dutch sides?

You may enquire for either side, but professional eligibility and property access must be checked separately.

Are the prices published?

Yes. See the live service menu for eight fixed massage options in USD. Group or custom arrangements are handled separately.

Can I book a hotel-room massage?

Only where the hotel permits visiting massage professionals and an appropriate private room is available.

Are couples massages available?

A simultaneous couples session requires two qualified professionals, two tables and adequate space. Availability must be confirmed.

How much notice is required?

Please enquire at least six hours ahead in local St Martin time, with additional notice when staffing or travel requires it.

Can I request a yacht or beach massage?

Only with appropriate venue or captain's permission and conditions suitable for professional treatment.

Do I pay when I submit the form?

No. This form does not charge your card, reserve an appointment or guarantee staffing.

Can I call?

Yes. Call or WhatsApp +1 (242) 816-8286, the central Tranquilitas concierge number.

Can I request a private massage around Marigot, Grand Case, Orient Bay & Terres Basses or Simpson Bay, Philipsburg & Maho?

Yes, you can send an enquiry for those locations. The area pages are planning guides, not a guarantee of therapist coverage. We check your exact property and availability before offering a time.

What should I prepare for a hotel, villa or group massage enquiry?

Choose a service and duration, provide the exact property, guest count and preferred local start time, and check whether outside therapists are allowed. Please allow at least six hours; additional notice may be needed.
